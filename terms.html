Terms of service

Practical information about requesting and attending lessons.

Using the site

Use the lesson information to explore subjects and request support. Please provide accurate contact details and submit only material you are entitled to share.

Requests and bookings

A request opens a conversation. We confirm the tutor, schedule, price, cancellation conditions and session format by email before you accept a booking.

Learning materials

Lesson material is provided for personal study. Ask before recording a session, sharing lesson resources or distributing another learner’s work.

Choosing a learning plan

Individual sessions and ongoing plans depend on tutor availability. Tell us your time zone, goal and preferred subject so we can suggest a suitable match.

Before your session

Check the time zone and joining instructions in your booking email. Have your learning materials ready and test your microphone and internet connection. If a link fails, reply to your booking email so the tutor can help.

Changes and concerns

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to move a session or discuss a concern. Your booking confirmation contains the conditions agreed for that lesson.
