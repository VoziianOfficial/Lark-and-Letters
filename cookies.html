Cookie policy

What this website saves in your browser and how to manage it.

On this pageYour policy preferenceStorage durationSite deliveryManaging your preferenceContact form and storage

Your policy preference

When you select “Got it”, this site stores a preference named lark-policy in your browser’s local storage. It lets us keep the notice closed on later visits.

Storage duration

The preference stays on this browser until you clear this site’s data. Clearing site data restores the notice on your next visit.

Site delivery

The teaching website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting or security services may process technical information when delivering the site.

Managing your preference

To bring the policy notice back, open your browser’s privacy settings and clear stored site data for this website. Your choice applies separately to each browser, device and private browsing session.

Contact form and storage

The notice preference contains only the value “yes”. Your name, email address and enquiry text are sent to the server when you submit the form. The form does not save these fields in the site’s local storage.
