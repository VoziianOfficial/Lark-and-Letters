Privacy policy

How we handle enquiries from learners, families and partners.

Information we receive

When you submit an enquiry, we receive your name, email address, topic and message. Please leave sensitive information out of your message. A parent or guardian should contact us on behalf of a young learner.

How we use your enquiry

We use the details you provide to reply, discuss availability, prepare a quote or respond to a partnership proposal. Our hosting and email providers process the technical data needed to deliver these services.

Retention and your choices

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond, manage any booking and meet applicable obligations. Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of your information.

Who receives your message

The enquiry form sends your details to the school’s configured email inbox. The reply address is the address you entered. Please check it carefully so that any response reaches you.

Booking for a child

Use a parent or guardian’s contact details. A subject, approximate level and learning goal are enough for the first conversation. Please leave school records, identity documents and private health details out of the form.

Contact

Send privacy questions to the site operator at the email below.
