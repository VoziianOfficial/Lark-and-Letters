Before you book

Find a starting level, prepare your materials and choose a time that works for you.

Ask a question
How do I choose a starting level?

Tell us what you can already do and where you get stuck. Your tutor can start with a short activity and adjust the lesson as you work.

Can I book just one lesson?

Yes. Choose a single session and describe the question, artwork or school task you want to bring.

What materials should I prepare?

For language practice, bring a topic. For maths, bring a problem and paper. For art, a pencil and sketchbook are enough to start.

How is the time confirmed?

Include your time zone and preferred days in the request. The team will reply by email with available options.

Can a parent request a lesson?

Yes. A parent or guardian can send the request and describe the learner’s goals and the support they need.
